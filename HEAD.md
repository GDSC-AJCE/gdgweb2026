- **Organization:** GDG AJCE (Google Developer Groups on Campus — Amal Jyothi College of Engineering).
- **Mission:** Empowering students with cutting-edge Google technologies (AI/ML, Gemini, Cloud, Android, Web, and Open Source).

## ⚡ 3. Platform Modules & Capabilities ("Many Options")

### A. Program & Event Architecture (`/programs`, `/programs/ongoing`, `/programs/past`)
- **All Programs (`/programs`):** Master directory with category and status filters.
- **Live & Ongoing Events (`/programs/ongoing`):** Dedicated page for hackathons, workshops, and sprints actively underway. Features live countdowns, live problem statements, check-in links, and active submissions.
- **Conducted & Past Events (`/programs/past`):** Comprehensive archive of concluded chapter events. Features recaps, winner showcases, recording links, slide decks, and participant certificate claims.
- **Interactive Registration (`/programs/[slug]`):** In-app registration workflow directly linked to Firestore with custom dynamic fields, slot limits, real-time RSVP counter, waitlisting, and verifiable QR tickets.
- **Verification Portal (`/verify/[id]`):** Public validator for event tickets and attendance badges.

### B. Competitions & Seasonal Leaderboards (`/leaderboard`, Landing Page Section)
- **Monthly & Seasonal Tracks:** Regular chapter competitions including Gemini AI Hack Jams, Cloud Study Jams, Android Sprints, and Google Solution Challenge.
- **Dynamic Leaderboard System:**
  - Filterable by Season (e.g., Season 2026, Autumn 2025) and Category (AI/ML, Cloud, Web/Android, Open Source).
  - Podium Cards (🥇 Gold, 🥈 Silver, 🥉 Bronze) highlighting top performers.
  - Searchable rankings table (name, department, points, solved challenges, badges).
  - Firestore collection `leaderboard` with real-time score updates.
- **Landing Page Preview:** Live seasonal leaderboard highlights embedded directly on the home page with quick CTA to full standings.

### C. Student & Member Portal (`/profile`)
- Google Single Sign-on (SSO) profile dashboard.
- Active and past registrations with downloadable digital tickets.
- Certificate repository (`/profile/certificates`) with unique credential verification IDs.
- Community badges, points, and event attendance track record.

### D. Executive & Core Administration Suite (`/core`, `/execom`)
- Protected role-based administration dashboard.
- **Event Management:** Create, edit, schedule, publish, and close registrations.
- **Attendee Management:** Real-time participant tracker, attendance check-in scanning via camera/QR, export to Excel/CSV.
- **Communication:** Automated registration confirmations and event update dispatches.
- **Credential Generation:** Dynamic issuance of attendance and achievement certificates.

### E. Innovation Labs & Technology Tracks
- **AI & Machine Learning Lab:** Gemini API, TensorFlow, JAX, Vertex AI.
- **Cloud & DevOps Lab:** Google Cloud Platform, Firebase, serverless architectures.
- **Web & Android Lab:** Flutter, Kotlin, modern Web technologies.
- **Open Source & Security Lab:** Open source contributions, Git workflows, security workshops.

### F. Core Institutional Pages (`/about`, `/contact`, `/gallery`, `/design-system`)
- **Landing Page (`/`):** Google Labs interactive experience, featured experiments, live seasonal leaderboard standings, and community tracks.
- **About Page (`/about`):** Chapter mission, pedagogy (Codelabs over lectures), student core leads, alumni cohorts, and faculty mentorship at Amal Jyothi College of Engineering.
- **Contact Page (`/contact`):** Direct desk messaging form linked to Firestore, campus hub coordinates at AJCE, and official community links.
- **Gallery (`/gallery`):** Photo and video memories from past GDG AJCE events.
- **Design System Portal (`/design-system`):** Interactive design standards, token copy tools, component catalog, and live playground.

### H. Weekly Tech Newsletter & Pulse Platform (`/newsletter`, `/newsletter/[slug]`, `/newsletter/unsubscribe`)
- **Master Newsletter Hub (`/newsletter`):** High-impact subscriber portal featuring custom track filters (AI/ML, Cloud, Web/Android, Open Source, Campus), 1-click subscription with instant welcome briefing dispatch, featured edition spotlight, and full historical issues archive.
- **Individual Issue Web Reader (`/newsletter/[slug]`):** Dedicated web reading experience with table of contents, GFM markdown rendering, social sharing cards (WhatsApp, X/Twitter, LinkedIn), and inline subscriber callouts.
- **Unsubscribe & Preference Management (`/newsletter/unsubscribe`):** CAN-SPAM compliant token-verified one-click unsubscription workflow.
- **Automated Weekly Tech Briefing Engine:** Multi-track update generator covering Google Gemini/Vertex AI, Cloud Run/Kubernetes, Android 15/Flutter 3.x, trending open-source tooling, and AJCE campus codelabs.
- **Email Delivery & Admin Broadcast Suite (`/admin`):** Direct integration with nodemailer and chapter SMTP engine for batch dispatching, test previews, subscriber CSV exports, and scheduled cron triggers (`/api/newsletter/cron`).
